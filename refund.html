Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

CartVanta is preparing its online ordering and fulfilment process. This policy explains how cancellation and refund requests will be handled once orders are accepted.

Cancellation requests

Contact us as soon as possible with your order reference. A cancellation may be possible before the order is processed or dispatched. Once a supplier has processed or shipped an order, cancellation may not be possible.

Refunds

If CartVanta confirms that a refund is due, it will be initiated to the original payment method where supported. The time taken for the amount to appear depends on the payment provider and bank. We will communicate the refund status.

Damaged, incorrect or missing items

Contact support with your order reference and clear photos or other relevant details. We will review the issue and explain the available resolution under the applicable order terms.

How to request help

Email fundvexxasupport@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 72909 77231. Include your order reference and the reason for your request.

Contact

For questions, contact fundvexxasupport@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 72909 77231.
