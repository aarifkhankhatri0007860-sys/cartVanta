Shipping Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

CartVanta is preparing its product catalogue and supplier fulfilment process. Shipping availability, charges, dispatch estimates and delivery timelines depend on the product, supplier warehouse and destination.

Before placing an order

The applicable shipping method, charges and estimated delivery window will be displayed or communicated before an order is confirmed. We will not promise a delivery date until it has been checked for the selected product and destination.

Tracking and delays

Where tracking is provided by the supplier or carrier, tracking details will be shared after dispatch. Customs checks, carrier disruptions and other events outside our control may affect delivery estimates.

Current store status

Online checkout is being prepared. Please contact our support team to confirm whether ordering is currently available. Do not make payment unless you receive an official CartVanta order/payment link.
