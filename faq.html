Good to know.

Are products shown in the shop live?

Product listings are retrieved from CJdropshipping. The store checks warehouse stock before displaying returned listings, but stock can change and must be reconfirmed before an order is accepted.

Are the prices shown final customer prices?

No. Current catalogue prices are supplier costs in USD. They are not final retail prices and do not include confirmed shipping, taxes, duties or CartVanta's final selling price.

Can I place and pay for an order right now?

Online checkout is still being prepared. Do not send payment unless CartVanta provides an official order confirmation and secure payment checkout.

Do you ship to India and the USA?

We are building support for both markets. Shipping availability, cost and estimated delivery depend on the product, warehouse and destination and must be checked before order confirmation.

How can I contact support?

Email fundvexxasupport@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 72909 77231.

How do I request a cancellation or refund?

Contact support with your order reference. Requests are reviewed under the published Cancellation & Refund Policy.
